# Job Hunter Marketing Starter Guide

This is a practical first campaign for introducing Job Hunter without overpromising or needing a marketing team. Start with one short interactive product demo, share it with a small audience, learn from responses, and improve it.

For ready-to-record materials, use the [step-by-step Supademo guide](./docs/demo/STEP-BY-STEP-SUPADEMO.md), [voice-over and captions](./docs/demo/VOICEOVER-STORYBOARD.md), [fictional demo resume](./docs/demo/DEMO-RESUME.md), and [fictional job posting](./docs/demo/DEMO-JOB-POSTING.md).

## 1. What Job Hunter does

**Positioning:** Job Hunter is a workspace for making a job search more organized and actionable.

**Short description:**

> Understand how your experience relates to a role, organize your applications, and turn skill gaps into practical next steps—all in one workspace.

**Good first audience:** People actively applying for jobs, early-career job seekers, and career changers who want a clearer process.

**The core story:** A job seeker finds a role, reviews how their experience relates to its requirements, saves the opportunity, and uses any gaps to plan a next step.

Job Hunter helps people prepare and organize. It does not guarantee a job or interview, make hiring decisions, or submit applications on a user's behalf.

## 2. Important launch check before promoting

The current billing integration is still **PayFast sandbox-only**. Do not market the paid plans as ready to purchase, direct customers to complete sandbox checkout, or describe sandbox payments as real subscriptions.

Before sharing the site widely:

- Confirm what a new visitor can actually use today, including sign-up and free access.
- Use a clear call to action such as **“Try the free pilot”** only if new users can currently sign up and use it. Otherwise use **“Request early access”** or **“Join the waitlist.”**
- Hide, disable, or clearly label any sandbox checkout links before sending prospects to the Plans & usage page. A customer-facing sandbox payment flow is for testing, not public sales.
- Keep billing enforcement and live payments off until live readiness is completed.
- Check the current plan limits in the deployed app before mentioning exact limits or prices. Starter limits recently changed in the code and may need to be deployed.

## 3. Make one Supademo

### Demo goal

Show one complete, useful workflow in about **60–90 seconds**. Avoid trying to tour every screen.

**Suggested demo title:** *From job post to a clear next step*

**Suggested opening text:**

> A job description can feel like a long list of requirements. Job Hunter helps you review the role, organize your application, and decide what to do next.

### Prepare a safe demo account

1. Use a separate test account, not your personal account or a real customer's account.
2. Fill it with fictional information, for example: **Amani Ndlovu**, aiming for **Junior Data Analyst**.
3. Use a fictional job post and fictional resume. Do not upload a real person's CV or contact details.
4. Remove or hide email addresses, account identifiers, browser notifications, private tabs, and unrelated personal data.
5. Avoid recording the checkout flow. The current PayFast experience is sandbox testing, not a public payment offer.
6. If AI consent appears, demonstrate it honestly; do not imply that a user's resume is processed without their choice.

### Capture these steps

Supademo's exact editor labels may differ. Use its free plan's available capture and sharing options; check its current limits before publishing.

1. Open the public homepage and introduce the problem in one sentence.
2. Show the **Applications** list and save the fictional role.
3. Open **Check job fit** and provide the fictional resume and job description. Show the consent step if it appears.
4. Show the fit review as guidance: strengths, possible gaps, and questions to verify—not a hiring prediction.
5. Show how a saved gap can lead to a roadmap or focused Proof Sprint.
6. Finish on the application list or dashboard, pointing out that the user stays in control and applies themselves.

For each step:

- Use one short caption that explains the benefit, not just the button.
- Keep the cursor movement deliberate and pause briefly after each important result.
- Crop or zoom the capture so text is readable on a phone.
- Keep the total demo to roughly 6–8 steps. Remove dead time, typing, loading waits, and repeated navigation.

### Caption text you can use

1. **Save the role:** “Keep the company, role, status, and next step together.”
2. **Review the match:** “Compare your experience with the job description.”
3. **Check the details:** “Review strengths, possible gaps, and questions before deciding.”
4. **Choose a next step:** “Turn a skill gap into a practical roadmap or project sprint.”
5. **Stay in control:** “Edit and verify AI-assisted drafts. You submit applications yourself.”

### Publish and test

1. Give the demo a clear title and add a short description.
2. Publish it using a share link if your Supademo plan allows.
3. Open that link in a private/incognito window and test it on a phone-sized screen.
4. Check every click, caption, and destination. Make sure the link does not expose your account or private data.
5. Put the demo link on the homepage or in a social post only after this check.

## 4. Ready-to-use marketing copy

### Short profile description

> Job Hunter helps you organize applications, review job fit, and turn skill gaps into practical next steps.

### Social post

> Job searching can become a lot to keep track of: roles to review, applications to follow up, and skills you may want to build.
>
> I’m building Job Hunter to bring those next steps into one workspace. You can organize applications, review how your experience relates to a job description, and use possible gaps to plan a roadmap or project sprint.
>
> It’s guidance—not a hiring prediction—and you stay in control of every application.
>
> Take a look at the short demo: [ADD SUPADEMO LINK]
>
> I’d love to hear: what part of your job search is hardest to keep organized?

### Short video voice-over

> “Found a role that looks interesting? Save it in Job Hunter, compare the job description with your experience, and review the strengths and gaps to think about. If you want, turn a gap into a practical roadmap or project sprint. Keep your applications and next steps together—and submit each application yourself. Job Hunter: a clearer job search starts here.”

### Call-to-action options

Choose **one** CTA that matches the real sign-up experience:

- If public sign-up is working: **Try the free pilot**
- If access is limited: **Request early access**
- If you are gathering feedback first: **Watch the demo and tell me what would help**

Do not use “Buy now,” “Subscribe,” or paid-plan promises while checkout is sandbox-only.

## 5. A simple first-week plan

| Day | Action | What to learn |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finish the demo and test it on desktop and phone | Is the workflow understandable without your explanation? |
| 2 | Share the social post with the demo link | Do people watch, click, or ask questions? |
| 3 | Ask 3–5 job seekers for a 10-minute reaction | Which problem feels most relevant to them? |
| 4 | Improve the demo or homepage based on one repeated point of confusion | Did the change make the value clearer? |
| 5 | Share a short clip or one screenshot with a specific question | Which feature or outcome gets a useful response? |
| 6 | Follow up with people who opted in to hear more | Would they use it, and what would they try first? |
| 7 | Review the numbers and choose one next experiment | What should you change—not just post more of? |

Respect group rules when sharing in communities. Ask for feedback instead of repeatedly posting promotional links. Message people directly only when they have invited or agreed to hear from you.

## 6. Keep score without overcomplicating it

Record these in a simple spreadsheet:

- Date and channel
- Post or demo link
- Views or impressions (if shown)
- Demo link clicks
- Sign-ups or early-access requests
- Useful questions or objections
- What you will change next

The first goal is not viral reach. Aim to learn whether the intended audience understands the problem, finds the workflow useful, and wants to try it.

## 7. Claims and privacy checklist

Before every post or demo, check:

- [ ] The feature shown exists in the deployed app.
- [ ] The demo contains fictional data or data used with explicit permission.
- [ ] No passwords, tokens, private URLs, customer records, or payment details are visible.
- [ ] AI output is described as something to review, not guaranteed to be correct.
- [ ] No employment, interview, or salary outcome is promised.
- [ ] It is clear that users submit applications themselves.
- [ ] Paid plans are not presented as live while checkout is sandbox-only.
- [ ] Prices and usage limits match the currently deployed billing page.
- [ ] Any testimonial or customer result is genuine and shared with permission.

## 8. Suggested next steps

1. Confirm public sign-up and the free pilot are ready for new visitors.
2. Make the sandbox/live billing status clear or hide sandbox checkout from marketing traffic.
3. Build and test the single Supademo workflow above.
4. Publish one post and invite a small group to give feedback.
5. Use what people say to improve the onboarding and demo before investing in ads.
