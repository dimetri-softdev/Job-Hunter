# Job Hunter Supademo: Step-by-Step Recording Guide

**Demo title:** From job post to a clear next step  
**Target length:** 60–90 seconds  
**Audience:** People actively applying for roles, early-career job seekers, and career changers  
**Demo account scenario:** Amani Ndlovu, a fictional candidate exploring a Junior Data Analyst role

The demo should show one story, not every feature: **review a role → compare experience → save the opportunity → choose a practical next step.**

## Before you record

### Prepare the demo account

1. Use a separate test account with fictional profile details. Do not record your personal account or a real user's information.
2. Set the profile name to **Amani Ndlovu** and the target role to **Junior Data Analyst**.
3. Use the fictional resume in [DEMO-RESUME.md](./DEMO-RESUME.md). Copy it into Word or Google Docs and save it as a text-based `.docx` or PDF. The fit-check upload accepts PDF, DOCX, JPEG, PNG, or WebP up to 5 MB.
4. Use the fictional posting in [DEMO-JOB-POSTING.md](./DEMO-JOB-POSTING.md). Paste its text into the job-description field; that field requires at least 80 characters.
5. Make sure the app is responsive and the test account can complete the fit check. Review the result before recording. AI results can vary, so narrate only what the screen actually shows.
6. Avoid checkout, billing settings, admin screens, browser notifications, and unrelated tabs. The PayFast integration is sandbox-only and is not part of this product demo.
7. Close private tabs and hide bookmarks, email addresses, account identifiers, and browser extensions that are not part of the story.

### Set up Supademo

1. Open the signed-in Job Hunter app in a clean browser window.
2. Set the browser zoom to about 100–110% so labels are readable.
3. Start Supademo's browser capture using the option available in your account.
4. Capture one action at a time. Pause briefly after each important screen so viewers can read it.
5. If Supademo offers step annotations, use the exact captions in [VOICEOVER-STORYBOARD.md](./VOICEOVER-STORYBOARD.md).

## Record these steps in order

| Step | What to do on screen | Caption | Target time |
|---|---|---|---:|
| 1. Set the context | Start on the signed-in Career dashboard or Applications page. Keep the view clean. | **A job search has a lot of moving parts.** | 5 sec |
| 2. Open Applications | Select **Job applications** in the left navigation. | **Keep each opportunity and its next step together.** | 7 sec |
| 3. Start a fit check | Choose **Check job fit**. | **Compare your experience with a role you’re considering.** | 6 sec |
| 4. Add the resume | Upload the prepared fictional resume. | **Start with the experience you already have.** | 7 sec |
| 5. Add the role | Paste the fictional Junior Data Analyst posting. Add **Cape Town Insights Lab** as the company and **Junior Data Analyst** as the role if those fields are shown. | **Add the job requirements you want to review.** | 8 sec |
| 6. Show consent | Pause at the AI-processing consent control. Read it and opt in only for this fictional demo. | **You choose when to request an AI-assisted review.** | 7 sec |
| 7. Run and review | Submit the fit check. Wait for the result. Scroll slowly through the actual strengths, gaps, and questions shown. Do not imply the result predicts hiring. | **Review relevant evidence, possible gaps, and questions to verify.** | 15 sec |
| 8. Save the opportunity | Choose **Save to applications**. Add the fictional company, role, and a simple next action such as “Review SQL reporting examples.” Save it. | **Save the role and decide what to do next.** | 10 sec |
| 9. Show a next step | If available for the saved result, open **Build proof sprint** or the roadmap action and show the generated tasks. Do not complete or claim a result you did not actually do. | **Turn a skill gap into a practical project plan.** | 12 sec |
| 10. Close | Return to the application or dashboard view. | **Stay in control: review, edit, and apply yourself.** | 6 sec |

### If you need a shorter demo

Use steps 1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 8, and 10. Keep the product story intact; skip the proof sprint rather than rushing through it.

### If a feature or result is unavailable

- If the app does not show a proof-sprint action for this result, finish after saving the application. Do not fake the interaction.
- If the fit result is unclear or incorrect, do not record it as a positive example. Adjust the fictional resume/posting or ask for feedback on the flow.
- If consent or a loading/error state appears unexpectedly, stop capture, resolve it, and record a clean run. Do not edit the video to imply an action succeeded when it did not.
- If the deployed Starter plan limits prevent the flow, use an authorized test setup; do not enable paid enforcement or live payments to record a demo.

## Edit the recording

1. Remove page loads, typing pauses, accidental clicks, and any loading time that does not explain the experience.
2. Keep the cursor visible and move it slowly. Avoid circling or clicking repeatedly.
3. Use one caption per step. Keep each caption to one short sentence.
4. Keep important result text on screen long enough to read. Do not crop out consent, uncertainty, or relevant limitations.
5. Use captions even if you also record voice-over; many viewers watch with sound off.
6. Keep the final demo between 60 and 90 seconds. Prefer clear over fast.
7. Add a final card with the appropriate call to action:
   - **Try the free pilot** only if public sign-up and free use are working.
   - Otherwise use **Watch the demo and share feedback** or **Request early access**.
8. Do not add a purchase CTA while PayFast is sandbox-only.

## Final quality check

Before sharing the link:

- [ ] Open the published Supademo link in a private/incognito window.
- [ ] Test it on a phone-sized screen and on desktop.
- [ ] Confirm every interactive step works and captions match the screen.
- [ ] Confirm no real resume, email, customer data, credentials, or account details are exposed.
- [ ] Confirm no sandbox checkout or payment page appears.
- [ ] Confirm claims are limited to what Job Hunter actually does.
- [ ] Ask one person unfamiliar with the product to watch without explanation. Ask: “What does this help you do?” If they cannot answer, simplify the opening and steps.

## Share it

Use the prepared post and narration in [VOICEOVER-STORYBOARD.md](./VOICEOVER-STORYBOARD.md). Start with a small audience, ask one specific feedback question, and improve the demo based on repeated confusion rather than trying to post everywhere at once.
