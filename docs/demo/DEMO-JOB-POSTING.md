# Fictional Demo Job Posting — Junior Data Analyst

**Demo-only fictional posting.** The company and role below are invented for the product demo.

## Junior Data Analyst

**Company:** Cape Town Insights Lab  
**Location:** Cape Town, South Africa · Hybrid  
**Employment:** Full-time · Entry level

### About the role

Cape Town Insights Lab is looking for a curious Junior Data Analyst to help the operations team turn everyday business data into clear, useful reporting. This is an entry-level role with mentorship from experienced analysts.

### What you will do

- Clean, validate, and organize operational datasets.
- Write basic SQL queries to answer recurring business questions.
- Prepare spreadsheet reports and simple charts.
- Help maintain dashboards and explain changes in key metrics.
- Document assumptions, data-quality issues, and analysis steps.
- Work with operations colleagues to understand their questions before analyzing data.

### What we are looking for

- Some experience with Excel or Google Sheets, including formulas and pivot tables.
- Basic SQL knowledge; familiarity with SELECT, filtering, grouping, and joins.
- Clear written communication and attention to detail.
- Willingness to ask questions and verify data before drawing conclusions.
- A portfolio, coursework project, or practical example is welcome.

### Helpful but not required

- Introductory Python for data analysis.
- Experience with Tableau, Power BI, or another dashboard tool.
- Familiarity with data privacy and responsible handling of customer information.

### How to apply

Please submit a resume and a short note describing one analysis or reporting task you have completed. The successful candidate will be asked to discuss their approach and the limitations of their example.
