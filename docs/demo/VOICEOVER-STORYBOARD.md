# Job Hunter Demo: Voice-over and Storyboard

Use this alongside [STEP-BY-STEP-SUPADEMO.md](./STEP-BY-STEP-SUPADEMO.md). This script describes the intended story; adapt the narration to the screens and result actually captured.

## 75-second voice-over

> “A job search can quickly turn into a lot of tabs, notes, and next steps.  
>   
> Job Hunter gives you one place to organize opportunities and review a role you’re considering. Add a resume and job description, then choose whether to request an AI-assisted fit review.  
>   
> The review helps you look at relevant experience, possible gaps, and questions to verify. It’s guidance—not a hiring prediction.  
>   
> Save the opportunity with a next action, then use a skill gap to explore a practical roadmap or project sprint.  
>   
> You stay in control: review the suggestions, decide what to do, and submit applications yourself. Job Hunter—a clearer job search starts here.”
