# JobHunter short promo video

## Format

- Length: about 30 seconds
- Frame: vertical 9:16 for Reels, Shorts, and status updates
- Style: use real captures from the deployed site, the existing JobHunter logo, and the site's dark background with blue/violet accents
- Audio: silent render; add the provided voice-over separately if desired. On-screen text carries the message without audio.
- End card: JobHunter logo, “A clearer job search starts here,” and the live homepage URL

## Voice-over

> “A job search has a lot of moving parts. JobHunter helps you keep opportunities and follow-ups together, review how your experience relates to a role, and turn possible skill gaps into practical next steps. Suggestions are guidance—not a hiring prediction. You stay in control and submit your applications yourself. JobHunter: a clearer job search starts here.”

## Shot list and captions

| Time | Picture | Voice-over / on-screen caption |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4s | Real capture of the live homepage hero. | “A job search has a lot of moving parts.” |

| 16–23s | Existing launch poster in the site's visual style. | “JobHunter is now live.” |
| 23–30s | Branded end card. | “A clearer job search starts here.” + “Get started” + website URL |

## Recording notes

- The homepage workspace preview is explicitly illustrative. Do not present it as a real dashboard or a real user's result.
- Capture signed-in product screens from a dedicated demo account with fictional data. Never show real resumes, contact information, account identifiers, payment details, or browser notifications.
- Keep each screen still long enough to read. Use simple cuts or subtle zooms rather than fast transitions.
- If an AI consent step appears, show it accurately. Do not imply that private information is processed without the user's choice.
- Do not show checkout or imply guaranteed employment outcomes.
